Our mission

Solis is a charity non-profit organization promoting awareness on the issue of adolescent mental health.

Learn more

Issues

Social media

Social media is destroying a generation. Studies have shown that heavy social media usage for teens is correlated with anxiety and depression, and glamorizes and promotes self-harm and suicide.

Parents / family

Poor parenting is shown to be directly associated with the mental well-being of adolescents. Negligence, abuse, poor relationship, and lack of attention to mental health from parents leads to youth depression.

Lack of resources

There is a shortage of mental health services available in the US. With the high rate of mental health disorders among adolescents, only about 1/5 has access to specialized mental health care.
