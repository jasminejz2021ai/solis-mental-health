Issues

Social media

Studies have shown that heavy social media use among teens is correlated with anxiety and depression, and that some content glamorizes self-harm and suicide.

Parents and family

Family relationships are closely tied to how adolescents are doing. Neglect, abuse, a strained relationship, or little attention to mental health can contribute to youth depression.

Lack of resources

There is a shortage of mental health services in the United States. Many adolescents with a mental health condition do not receive specialized care.
