Solis

Mission

Reducing stigma around youth mental health through peer education, community outreach, and resource promotion.

Solis is a youth-led nonprofit promoting awareness of adolescent mental health and advocating for young people to be met with support instead of silence.

The work follows three paths:

Peer education

Free presentations for secondary schools on why mental health matters, what can make it worse, how to ask for help, and what the numbers show.

Community outreach

Talks, fairs, and events that bring students, families, educators, and local organizations into the same conversation.

Resource promotion

Introducing schools and students to mental health services that work with education programs, including certified teen therapy platforms.

Solis also speaks about pressures that show up often for teenagers, including heavy social media use, strained family support, and the shortage of mental health professionals.
