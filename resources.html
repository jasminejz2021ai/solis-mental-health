Mental health resources

Brightline

Brightline is a virtual behavioral health service built to care for children, teenagers, and their families, with family-focused support and coordinated care.

allcove

allcove is a space for youth to find community, support, advice, or a moment of pause. Services include support in mental health, peer and family, and education and employment.

Synergy eTherapy

Offers personalized online therapy services for teens and adults in 15 states.

Trust Mental Health

Trust Mental Health matches people with therapists, including BIPOC therapists, and offers in-person sessions in San Jose, Fremont, Mountain View, Palo Alto, and Campbell, plus virtual sessions across California.
