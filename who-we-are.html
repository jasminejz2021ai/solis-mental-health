Our mission

Solis is a youth-led non-profit organization promoting awareness on and advocating for adolescent mental health.

Specifications

To carry out our objective, we will strive to challenge and influence society to be more mindful and supportive about teenagers' mental health status, and commit more to stopping the mental health crisis. We will educate and spread our ideas about the causes of poor mental health, targeting the overuse of social media and poor parenting, and advocating for the crisis of shortages of mental health professionals.

To share our mission with secondary schools, we will provide free presentations on the importance of mental health, what is harmful and contributes to its decrease, how it affects society and their own future, how to get the help that they need, and provide statistics on the subject to all districts and schools in need. We will also reach out to the districts and schools where statistics show high mental crisis rates. Furthermore, we will introduce and connect districts and schools to mental health resources that specifically partner with education programs.

To support adolescents in need of mental health support, our organization will partner with certified online teen therapy from professional platforms to help adolescents connect with services most compatible and useful to them.
