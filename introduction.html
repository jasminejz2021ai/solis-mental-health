Introduction

Youth mental health is a public concern, and many students still struggle in private.

Adolescence is a time of fast change. School pressure, friendships, family life, and time online all shape how young people feel. When distress stays unspoken, students are less likely to use the help that already exists at school or in the community. That silence is the stigma Solis is trying to reduce.

United States high schools, 2023

What the CDC survey found

These figures come from the CDC Youth Risk Behavior Survey of U.S. high school students. They describe the past year. They are not a diagnosis of any one person.

39.7%reported persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness
28.5%reported poor mental health
20.4%seriously considered attempting suicide
9.5%attempted suicide

Source: CDC MMWR, Youth Risk Behavior Survey, United States, 2023. The same report found that connection at school, at home, and in the community was associated with lower risk.
