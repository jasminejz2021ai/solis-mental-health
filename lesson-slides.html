Lesson slides

Free lesson slides

A lot of the social kindness and mental health lessons in schools are very shallow and ineffective. They are brief and only state the obvious, and do not emphasize the importance of mental health and the ongoing crisis enough. Solis took the matter into our own hands and created a free lesson presentation for schools on educating adolescents about mental health. We focus on the causes and back up our claims with statistics to let adolescents realize how serious and relevant the issue is.
